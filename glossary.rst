    ************
    Nomenclature
    ************

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   AACE
      Advancement of Cost Engineering.

   ABC
      Activity Based Costing.

   ACS
      Attitude Determination and Control System.

   AEB
      *Agência Espacial Brasileira*, or Brazilian Space Agency.

   ARGOS
      Advanced Research and Global Observation Satellite.

   CBS
      Cost Breakdown Structure.

   CBT
      Cost Breakdown Tables.

   CDR
      Critical Design Review.

   CER
      Cost Estimation Report.

   CONOPS
      Concept of Operations.

   DCP
      Data Collection Platform.

   ECSS
      European Cooperation for Space Standardization.

   EDC
      Environmental Data Collector.

   EMMN
      *Estação Multimissão de Natal*, or Natal Multi-Mission Station in english.

   EPS
      Electric Power System.

   FEC
      Forward Error Correction.

   IoT
      Internet of Things.

   IIoT
      Industrial Internet of Things.

   INPE
      *Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais*, or National Institute for Space Research.

   INPE-COENE
      *Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais - Coordenação Espacial do Nordeste*, or National Institute for Space Research - Northeast Space Coordination.

   ISI-SE
      *Instituto SENAI de Inovação em Sistemas Embarcados*, or SENAI Institute for Innovation in Embedded Systems.

   ID
      Identification Data.

   LEO
      Low Earth Orbit.

   MA
      Mission Analysis.

   MDD
      Mission Definition Document.

   MDR
      Mission Definition Review.

   OBDH
      On-Board Data Handling.

   OBC
      On-Board Computer.

   TTC
      Telemetry, Tracking and Command.

   PCB
      Printed Circuit Board.

   SBCD
      *Sistema Brasileiro de Coleta de Dados*, or Brazilian Data Collection System.

   SINDA
      *Sistema Nacional de Dados Ambientais*, or National System of Environmental Data.

   SRR
      System Requirements Review.

   T2F
      Thermal Fluid Flow Group.

   TLE
      Two-Line Elements.

   TBD
      To Be Defined.

   TBC
      To Be Confirmed.

   TRL
      Technology Readiness Level.

   UFSC
      *Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina*, or Federal University of Santa Catarina.

   WBS
      Work Breakdown Structure.

   WP
      Work Package.

   IADC
      Inter-Agency Space Debris Coordination.

   VHF
      Very High Frequency.

   UHF
      Ultra High Frequency.

   DM
      Deployment Mode.

   IM
      Initialization Mode.

   NM
      Nominal Mode.

   SBM
      Stand-By Mode.

   CM
      Contingency Mode.

   SS
      Space System.

   CDS
      CubeSat Design Specification.

   TML
      Total Mass Loss.

   CVCM
      Collected Volatile Condensable Material.

   RTC
      Real Time Clocks.

   DoD
      Depth of Discharge.

   RTD
      Resistance Temperature Detector.

   MPP
      Maximum Power Point.

   BAT4C
      Battery Module 4C.

   RBF
      Remove Before Flight.

   IIP
      Interstage Interface Panels.

   AIT
      Assembly, Integration, and Testing.
